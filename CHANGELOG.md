# Changelog

All notable changes to this blueprint are documented here. The format loosely
follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/), versions follow
[SemVer](https://semver.org/).

## [v0.1.0] - unreleased

First release.

### Added

- **Refuel reminder** while the recommendation is "Refuel today": when a person
  or tracker leaves a place, when the car starts moving or at set times. Quiet
  once the tank is above a level or an optional helper says you have
  refuelled. Buttons *Refuelled* and *Later*, a message with today's and
  tomorrow's price and the saving of a full tank, own texts and actions.
- **Navigate button**: petrol stations nearby or your own station, in Google
  Maps, Apple Maps or Waze.
- **Own actions** on the integration's events: price announced or corrected,
  new price in effect, recommendation changed, price below a target.
- Six languages: English, German, French, Lëtzebuergesch, Português, Italiano.
